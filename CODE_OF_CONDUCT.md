# Code of Conduct

We welcome contributors regardless of background, experience, identity, or ability. Treat others with respect and help keep discussions constructive.

## Expected behavior

- Give specific, respectful feedback about the work.
- Be considerate of different experience levels and perspectives.
- Accept corrections and take responsibility for mistakes.
- Respect privacy and keep personal or confidential information out of public discussions.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, personal attacks, unwanted sexual attention, and publishing someone else's private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other community spaces managed by the project. Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close discussions, or restrict participation. Responses should be proportionate to the conduct and its impact.

Report concerns privately to a repository maintainer using an available private contact channel. If no channel is listed, ask a maintainer for one without posting personal details or allegations publicly. Maintainers should handle reports discreetly, avoid conflicts of interest, and explain moderation decisions to those affected when appropriate.
